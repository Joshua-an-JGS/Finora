FINORA — Institutional Finance & Payroll Management Platform
Standalone Windows Desktop Application (64-bit)
========================================================================

QUICK INSTALLATION INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Double-click "FINORA_Setup.exe" to begin installation.
2. Choose your installation destination or accept the standard default.
3. Finish the installer. FINORA will launch automatically.
4. On first-time launch, FINORA presents the "Workspace Setup" screen:
   - Enter your Organization / Company Name
   - Enter your Administrator Full Name & Work Email
   - Choose your Administrator Username (default is "admin")
   - Create and confirm a secure password (min 8 characters)
5. Click "Initialize FINORA Workspace".
6. Copy and safely store your cryptographic Emergency Recovery Key.
7. Sign in with your Administrator credentials to begin managing payroll!

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS:
- Windows 10 (1903+) or Windows 11 (64-bit)
- No external database or server required.
- Everything runs 100% locally on your computer with complete privacy.

UNINSTALLATION NOTE:
- If you ever uninstall FINORA, the uninstaller will completely and cleanly
  remove all application files, databases, and cached sessions.
- Any subsequent reinstallation will start 100% clean with the First-Time
  Account Creation screen.
